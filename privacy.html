Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. My Scheduler is built to help students organise their academic life while keeping personal information secure and under your control.

Effective Date: July 26, 2026

Introduction

Thank you for using My Scheduler. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our application.

My Scheduler is designed with privacy in mind. Most features work entirely offline, and your academic data remains stored on your own device.

Information Stored

The app may locally store information including:

  • Class schedules
  • Subjects
  • Attendance records
  • Tasks
  • Examinations
  • Holiday calendar
  • Reminder settings
  • Notes
  • User preferences

This information is stored only on your device unless you intentionally export or share it.

Personal Information

My Scheduler does not require account registration.

  • No Name Collection
  • No Email Collection
  • No Phone Number Collection
  • No Financial Information
  • No Government ID Collection
  • No Contact Collection
  • No Location Tracking

Notifications

The application uses Android local notifications to remind you about classes, assignments, exams, attendance goals and custom reminders.

These notifications are generated directly on your device and are not sent through our servers.

Permissions Used

  • Notification Permission
  • Exact Alarm Permission (if supported by your Android version)
  • Schedule Exact Alarm Permission (if applicable)

These permissions are only used to provide reminder functionality.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your personal information with third parties.

All academic information remains under your control on your own device.

Security

Reasonable security measures are used to protect the application's stored information. Since your data remains on your own device, keeping your phone protected with a secure lock screen is recommended.

Delete Your Data

You may remove your data at any time by:

  • Deleting records inside the app
  • Clearing app storage from Android Settings
  • Uninstalling My Scheduler
