Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 05, 2026. This Privacy Policy governs your use of My Scheduler.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal identify profiles automatically. The data parsed through your college timetables (such as course names, professors, room schedules) is stored entirely inside your local sandbox filesystem. When using local manual photo uploads, data is processed securely.

2. How We Use Information

All structured academic entries we process are utilized to render visual calendars, calculate current academic ratios, generate localized task timers, and organize grades in the CGPA module.

3. AI Features

For the AI Timetable and Academic Holiday imports, images and document blobs are transmitted to secure Gemini endpoints over HTTPS encryption. These document slices are processed transitively to resolve tables into JSON; they are never persisted or utilized for model-training pipelines.

4. Analytics

We leverage localized diagnostic logs and crash diagnostics to isolate threading issues. These diagnostics contain no personal variables, schedules, or academic grades.

5. Advertising

My Scheduler features clean, optional non-personalized advertisements which are restricted from accessing calendars or reading your stored course metrics.

6. Data Security

Your local databases are encrypted using native Android sandbox protection, making your data inaccessible to secondary third-party apps on your phone.

7. Children's Privacy

Our academic companion complies with COPPA. We do not gather or maintain files from users under the age of 13 knowingly.

8. Permissions Used

The applet requests CAMERA permission (to snap timetable boards), STORAGE permission (to select calendar PDFs), and NOTIFICATION permission (to trigger class and homework alert timers).

9. Third-party Services

We work with trusted services including Google Play Services, Firebase Crashlytics, and Gemini extraction APIs to drive continuous system updates.

10. Data Retention

All academic schedules are preserved indefinitely on your local smartphone. Removing the application purges all local databases automatically.

11. Contact Information

For any queries relating to student telemetry or storage encryptions, contact us at: support@myscheduler.app.

12. Changes to Policy

We reserve the right to revise this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the application indicates full acceptance of revised terms.
