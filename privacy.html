1. Introduction

My Scheduler is an offline personal academic planner that helps students organise classes, attendance, exams, assignments, tasks, notes, reminders, and schedules. The App follows a privacy-first approach: most features run entirely on-device, and your information stays under your control. By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. Scope

This policy applies only to the Android app My Scheduler (com.myscheduler.app), covering data processing, local storage, permissions, security, user rights, retention, import/export, and contact information. It does not apply to any third-party site or service.

3. Privacy by Design

  • No mandatory registration
  • No cloud account required
  • No remote database
  • No online profile
  • No behavioural tracking
  • No ad or analytics SDKs
  • No unnecessary permissions
  • No sale of user data
  • Local-first storage

4. Information We Collect

The App lets you create and manage: display name (optional), semester and subject details, timetable, attendance records, lecture and lab schedules, assignments, tasks, notes, holidays, exam schedules, reminder preferences, attendance goals, and app/theme/notification settings. All of it is entered by you and stored only in the App's local Room Database — the developer cannot access it remotely. An optional display name is never uploaded, shared, or used for advertising.

5. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Full legal name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Government ID / Aadhaar / PAN
  • Banking / card / UPI info
  • Passwords
  • Contacts, SMS, call history
  • Calendar events
  • Location / GPS data
  • Microphone or camera data
  • Biometric or health data
  • Browsing / search history
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Social media accounts

The App collects only what is necessary to provide the features you request — your timetable, attendance, reminders, and notes belong to you.

6. Local Data Storage

All data — subjects, attendance, timetable entries, reminders, tasks, exams, notes, holidays, and settings — is stored locally in a Room Database on your device. The developer has no ability to access, read, copy, or modify it remotely.

7. No Internet Communication

Core features work without an internet connection. No app data, attendance records, notes, or reminders are uploaded or synchronised to developer servers, and no background communication occurs for analytics or advertising. Any future online feature will be disclosed here before release.

8. Permissions

Requested: Notification permission, used only to show reminders you create (classes, exams, assignments, attendance, study reminders). Notifications are generated on-device and never transmitted.

Not requested:

  • Camera, microphone
  • Contacts, SMS, phone
  • Location, calendar
  • Bluetooth / nearby devices
  • Body sensors, activity recognition

9. Import and Export

The optional backup feature is user-initiated. Exported files are saved to a location you choose and never reach the developer or any server. Imported files are read and merged locally only. You are responsible for safeguarding any exported backup files.

10. Data Security

Safeguards include local storage via Room Database, no developer-managed remote database, no automatic data transmission, least-privilege permissions, and an offline-first architecture. No method guarantees absolute security — keep your device and screen lock updated, and manage exported backups securely.

11. Retention and Deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete individual entries, clear app data via Android Settings, import a replacing backup, or uninstall the App. The developer holds no copies and cannot recover deleted data. Since no remote database exists, there is nothing to delete on the developer's side.

12. No User Accounts

No registration is required — no username, password, email/phone verification, or social login (Google, Facebook, Apple) is needed to use any feature.

13. Third-Party Services and Advertising

The App does not integrate AdMob, Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, FCM), Google/Facebook sign-in, or any third-party analytics/advertising SDK. It shows no banner, interstitial, or rewarded ads, and does not profile users or sell data for advertising. Any future integration will be disclosed here in advance.

14. Children's Privacy

My Scheduler is built for students organising academic activity and is not directed at children below the age of consent under applicable law. Since data stays on-device with no registration, the developer does not knowingly receive personal information from children. Contact us if you believe otherwise.

15. International Users

Because user data is never transmitted to developer servers, no personal information is intentionally transferred across borders. Users remain responsible for complying with local laws.

16. Google Play Compliance

The developer complies with applicable Google Play policies on privacy, data transparency, minimisation, permissions, security, and consent. The Play Store's Data Safety section reflects the App's current practices.

17. User Rights

Since your data lives on your own device, you directly control accessing, correcting, exporting, or deleting it without needing to contact the developer. These actions are carried out within the App or via device settings.

18. Future Features

If features such as cloud sync, accounts, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or subscriptions are introduced, this policy will be updated beforehand, with any new data practices disclosed.

19. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised to reflect app improvements or legal requirements. The Effective Date will be updated and the latest version published at the policy URL above. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance.

20. Liability and Governing Law

The App is provided "as is." The developer is not liable for loss of local data, device malfunction, user error, backup corruption, unauthorised device access, or force majeure events. Regular backups via the export feature are recommended. This policy is governed by the laws of India, with disputes subject to Indian courts.
